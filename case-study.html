Case study

From scattered tasks to organized systems.

Real estate operations and marketing support for a busy U.S. real estate professional.

Residential Real EstateOperations & VAOngoing Engagement

The challenge

Leads, transactions, marketing activities, and recurring administrative work needed clearer ownership, better tracking, and more consistent follow-through.

The approach

Centralize information, document repeatable processes, maintain the CRM, coordinate transaction details, and keep marketing tasks moving through a dependable workflow.

CRM & lead management

  • Contact and lead organization
  • Lead tagging and database maintenance
  • Follow-up organization and tracking

Transaction support

  • ZipForm transaction management
  • DocuSign coordination
  • RentSpree and lease documentation
  • Transaction detail verification

Marketing & social media

  • Instagram and TikTok content
  • Listing promotions and campaigns
  • Open house and Just Sold marketing
  • Community engagement

Operations & systems

  • KPI and engagement tracking
  • Content planning and scheduling
  • SOP development
  • Workflow organization
